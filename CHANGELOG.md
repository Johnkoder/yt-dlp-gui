# Changelog

## v0.1.0 - 2026-09-06

### Added

- Video and audio downloads
- Video quality selection
- Audio conversion to MP3, M4A, WAV, and FLAC
- Custom output folders
- FIFO download queue
- Download cancellation
- Playlist support
- Persistent download history
- Dependency detection for yt-dlp, Deno, and FFmpeg
- Windows MSI and NSIS packaging
